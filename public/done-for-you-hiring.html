Frequently asked questions

Is Done-For-You Hiring different from posting a job myself?

Yes. A regular $29 job post relies on you reviewing applicants and running interviews yourself. Done-For-You Hiring is exactly what it sounds like: our team sources, screens, and runs first-round interviews for each hire you need — whether that's one person or several — so you're not sifting through resumes or interviewing candidates yourself. You review a ready-to-hire shortlist and make the final call.

Do you interview candidates for me?

Yes. Our team sources candidates and runs first-round interviews, which we record — so you can simply pick from the shortlist, or watch the recordings and take any candidate further with your own interview. Either way, we only introduce candidates we're confident have what it takes to succeed in the role.

Do you check references or run background checks?

Yes. We check references and run background verification on every candidate we introduce — we verify it ourselves, we don't just take a candidate's word for it.

Are candidates equipped to work right away?

Yes. We confirm each candidate has a proper workstation and the right technology for the role — including things like a noise-cancelling headset — before they start, so they're work-ready from day one, not just claiming to be.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

Whatever the role needs, we actively support your new hire through the first 30 days. If a placement still doesn't work out in that window, we'll source a replacement at no additional cost.

Do I need to be hiring multiple people to use this?

No. Done-For-You Hiring works whether you need one hire or several in the same role — the process is the same either way, and pricing is per hire, so there's no minimum headcount.

Who handles it if I need to let someone go later?

We do. If it's ever time to part ways with a Done-For-You Hiring placement, tell us and we'll handle the offboarding conversation directly — you don't have to have that difficult talk yourself.

How is Done-For-You Hiring priced?

One flat fee per hire, paid in two parts — no subscription, no recurring cost, ever. An initial deposit, taken from that total fee, greenlights the search once we confirm scope after your intake; the remaining balance is due once your hire is confirmed.

What roles does Done-For-You Hiring cover?

Common requests include sales, intake/front desk, medical billing, customer support, IT support, and data entry — but any role in our regular hire-by-role list can be sourced this way. Tell us the role and headcount in the intake form.

Can Done-For-You Hiring move fast if I need someone urgently?

Yes — tell us your timeline in the intake form and we'll prioritize accordingly. Done-For-You Hiring exists partly for urgent hiring needs where you don't have weeks to run a process yourself.
