Questions

You probably want to know.

Is this different from posting a job on WorkBase PH?
Yes. Posting a job connects you with a specialist you hire and manage yourself. This is our own team building and running the work directly — you're not managing anyone.
Do you handle ongoing work, or just the initial build?
Depends on the service. Website/brand work and Automation are one-time projects — you own what we build. SEO and growth support is billed monthly since it's ongoing work. That's where the compounding results come from.
What if I already have a website?
No problem — our growth team can take over SEO and paid growth without touching your existing site, or refresh parts of it as needed.
How much does this cost?
It depends on scope, so we'd rather quote you accurately than guess here. Fill out our quick intake form and one of our specialists will follow up with a tailored quote and a discovery call.
How is pricing structured?
Website and brand work is a fixed project fee, scoped to what we agree on during your discovery call — no hourly billing. Automation works the same way: a flat, one-time fee per outcome, like a clock in/out kiosk or an intake workflow. SEO and growth support is a monthly retainer covering an agreed set of deliverables. If you run paid ads, ad spend goes directly to your own ad account — it's never part of our fee.
Do I have to take the full package?
No — take everything or pick just one service. Plenty of founders start with only a website, or only Automation, and add the rest once it's paying off.
Is this an on-call service?
No — this is a managed service on a set cadence, not real-time or 24/7 support. Expect scheduled updates and a regular reporting call, with replies to messages typically within 1–2 business days. Website builds include a set number of revision rounds; requests outside what we scoped together are quoted separately or slotted into the next cycle rather than handled ad hoc.
