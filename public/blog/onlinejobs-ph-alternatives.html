OnlineJobs.ph Alternatives: 5 Best Options (2026)

By WorkBase PH · Updated September 2026 · 9 min read

OnlineJobs.ph is the biggest name in Filipino hiring, but "biggest" isn't the same as "best fit for you." If you're here, you've probably already run into one of its limitations — the monthly subscription, the lack of any vetting, or the hours it takes to sort through hundreds of self-reported profiles to find a handful worth messaging.

Here are five real alternatives, what each one actually does differently, and how to tell which one fits the way you hire.

Why Employers Look for an OnlineJobs.ph Alternative

  • You pay whether or not you hire. The $69–$99/month subscription runs whether you find someone in week one or week six.
  • There's no vetting. Every profile is exactly as good as the person made it sound — inflated skills and outdated availability are common.
  • Scam job posts are a known problem. Because anyone can post without verification, experienced Filipino workers are increasingly cautious about which listings they trust.
  • Screening is entirely manual. There's no matching — you're the one filtering hundreds of applications down to a shortlist.

1. WorkBase PH — Best for Vetted, Matched Hiring

WorkBase PH is the closest thing to a direct replacement for OnlineJobs.ph's core job — connecting you with Filipino talent — while fixing the two things employers complain about most: no vetting and no way to judge a candidate before messaging them.

Instead of browsing a static database, you post a job once and the platform's AI matching surfaces candidates whose skills and experience actually fit. Most profiles include a video or audio introduction, an internet speed test, and a work-style assessment, so you can shortlist based on more than a self-written bio. Employers are verified before they can post, which is also why Filipino specialists tend to trust the listings here more than on an open board.

Cost: $29 flat for one job post — full applicant access from day one, no subscription, nothing to cancel.

2. VirtualStaff.ph — Best for Fully Managed Hiring

VirtualStaff.ph sits between a job board and a staffing agency: it helps source candidates and then handles contracts, payroll, and HR admin under its own system. If what frustrates you about OnlineJobs.ph is the administrative side of employing someone in the Philippines — not just finding them — this is the alternative that removes that burden, at a higher monthly cost and with less direct control over the relationship.

3. Upwork (Philippines filter) — Best for Project Work

Upwork isn't Filipino-specific, but filtering by Philippines gets you a large pool of freelancers with built-in contracts, time tracking, and payment protection. It's a poor substitute for OnlineJobs.ph on long-term hires — the 5% employer fee plus the 10–20% workers pay pushes rates up 15–25% over direct hiring — but for a one-off project, the protections are worth the premium.

4. PinoyMatch — Best for a Simpler, Smaller Board

PinoyMatch is a smaller, VA-focused marketplace with basic vetting and a cleaner interface than a large job board. The pool is far smaller than OnlineJobs.ph's, but for a first-time employer hiring a standard VA role, the simplicity can be worth the trade-off.

5. Facebook VA Groups — Best Free Option

Groups like "Filipino Virtual Assistants" let you post for free to an active community. There's no vetting, no payment protection, and no structure — you're doing all the screening yourself — but for low-stakes, one-off tasks, it costs nothing to try.

How to Choose

Hiring occasionally and want it done right the first time? WorkBase PH's flat fee and built-in vetting beat a subscription you'll barely use.

Hiring at high volume every month, or need payroll handled? OnlineJobs.ph's volume or VirtualStaff.ph's managed service scale better than a per-post model.

Short, one-off project? Upwork's contracts and payment protection are worth the extra fee for a single engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best alternative to OnlineJobs.ph?

It depends on what OnlineJobs.ph is missing for you. For vetted, matched candidates with video and audio intros instead of a self-reported profile, WorkBase PH is the closest direct alternative. For fully managed payroll and contracts, VirtualStaff.ph fills that gap. For short, project-based work with built-in payment protection, Upwork is a better fit than a job board.

Why are employers switching away from OnlineJobs.ph?

The most common reasons are the monthly subscription (you pay whether or not you hire that month), zero vetting on profiles, a well-documented scam-job-post problem that discourages experienced Filipino workers from applying, and the time cost of manually screening a huge, unfiltered pool of applicants.

Is there a free alternative to OnlineJobs.ph?

Facebook groups for Filipino virtual assistants let you post for free and have active membership, but there's no vetting or payment protection, so screening is entirely on you. Most paid alternatives — including WorkBase PH's flat per-post fee — exist precisely to remove that screening burden.

What's the cheapest alternative if I'm only hiring once or twice this year?

A monthly subscription rarely makes sense for occasional hiring — you're paying for months you don't need. WorkBase PH's $29 flat fee for one job post, with no subscription, is typically the cheapest option for employers who hire only once or twice a year.

Do OnlineJobs.ph alternatives protect against scam job posts?

It varies by platform. OnlineJobs.ph allows anyone to post without verification, which is why fake job posts are a recurring complaint in its community. WorkBase PH verifies every employer before they can post a job, specifically to protect Filipino freelancers from scam listings — a factor worth checking on any platform you're considering.
