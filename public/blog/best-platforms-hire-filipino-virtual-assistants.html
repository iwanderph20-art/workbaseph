5 Best Platforms to Hire Filipino Virtual Assistants in 2026

By WorkBase PH · May 2026 · 8 min read

Not all Filipino hiring platforms work the same way. Some are simple job boards where you browse resumes. Others vet candidates for you. Some charge employers a subscription, others take a cut from the worker's pay. Knowing the difference saves you time, money, and a lot of frustration.

Here's an honest breakdown of the top 5 platforms for hiring Filipino virtual assistants and remote workers in 2026 — including how they work, what they cost, and which type of employer they suit best.

#2

OnlineJobs.ph

OnlineJobs.ph is the largest and oldest Filipino job board, with hundreds of thousands of profiles. You pay a monthly subscription to browse and message candidates directly. There's no vetting — anyone can create a profile — so the quality varies significantly.

The volume is high, which means you'll find candidates for almost any role. But you'll also spend significant time filtering through outdated profiles, unresponsive applicants, and candidates who are already employed. The lack of structured vetting puts the screening burden entirely on the employer.

Pros:
Massive candidate pool
Long-established platform
Affordable monthly fee
Good for niche roles
Cons:
No vetting or matching
Many inactive profiles
Time-intensive to screen
No video introductions
Best for: Experienced remote hirers comfortable doing their own screening who want maximum candidate volume.
#3

VirtualStaff.ph

VirtualStaff.ph is a managed staffing platform that handles contracts, payroll processing, and HR admin for Filipino remote workers. It sits between a pure job board and a full staffing agency. You post a role, they help source candidates, and the platform handles the employment infrastructure.

The main appeal is compliance and simplicity — everything is formalised under their system. The tradeoff is that it's more expensive than self-managed hiring and you have less direct control over the relationship with your worker.

Pros:
Payroll and contracts handled
Compliance-friendly structure
Good for teams scaling quickly
Cons:
Higher total cost
Less direct employer-worker relationship
Service fees on top of salary
Best for: Businesses that want the employment infrastructure managed for them and are okay paying a premium for it.
#4

Upwork (Philippines filter)

Upwork isn't specifically a Filipino hiring platform, but filtering by Philippines gives you access to a large pool of verified freelancers. Upwork handles contracts, time tracking, and payments. The hourly rates tend to be higher than direct hiring because workers factor in Upwork's commission structure.

Upwork works best for short-term or project-based work. For full-time or long-term hires, the platform fees (5% for employers, 10–20% from freelancers) make it significantly more expensive than alternatives.

Pros:
Built-in contracts and payment
Verified work history
Good for project-based work
Cons:
Expensive for full-time hires
Workers charge higher rates to cover Upwork fees
Not focused on PH talent specifically
Best for: Short-term or project-based work where you want payment protection and work history verification.
#5

PinoyMatch

PinoyMatch is a smaller, niche platform focused specifically on VA roles for small businesses. It does basic vetting and offers a curated feel compared to larger job boards. The platform is simpler to use than OnlineJobs.ph and has a tighter focus on VA-specific roles.

Pros:
Clean, simple interface
VA-focused
Reasonable pricing
Cons:
Smaller talent pool
Limited role variety
Less robust matching
Best for: First-time employers hiring for a standard VA role who want a simpler experience than a large job board.

Which Platform Should You Use?

The right platform depends on three things:

  • How much time you have to screen candidates. If you're busy running a business, platforms that pre-vet and match (like WorkBase PH) save you hours per hire.
  • Whether this is long-term or project-based. For ongoing roles, direct hiring platforms beat Upwork on total cost. For short projects, Upwork's built-in protections are worth it.
  • Your budget. The cheapest platform per month isn't always cheapest per hire. A $60/month job board that takes 20 hours to find one good hire is more expensive than a $150/month platform that matches you in a day.
