Free Filipino VA Contract Template (2026) — Copy, Edit & Use

By WorkBase PH · May 2026 · 5 min read

One of the most common mistakes employers make when hiring a Filipino virtual assistant is skipping a written contract. A short, clear agreement protects both sides — it sets expectations, prevents disputes over scope and payment, and gives your VA confidence that the engagement is professional and legitimate.

Below is a free, plain-language contract template you can copy, edit with your specifics, and send to your new hire. It covers everything a typical VA engagement needs without unnecessary legal complexity.

Note: This template is for independent contractor arrangements — the most common structure when hiring Filipino remote workers internationally. It is not a template for employment (with payroll, benefits, or statutory deductions). If you need a formal employment contract, consult a local HR or legal professional.

What a Good VA Contract Should Cover

Before we get to the template, here's what every Filipino VA contract should include at minimum:

  • Scope of work — What exactly will they do? How many hours per week?
  • Rate and payment schedule — How much, in what currency, paid how often?
  • Communication expectations — When are they available? How do you check in?
  • Confidentiality — Protection for your business data and client information
  • Intellectual property — Work produced belongs to the employer
  • Termination terms — How either party can end the arrangement

Free Filipino VA Contract Template

Copy the template below, fill in the highlighted fields, and share it with your VA via Google Docs or email for signature.

INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

Virtual Assistant Services

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Date] between:

Client: [Your Full Name or Company Name], located at [Your Country / City] ("Client")

Contractor: [VA's Full Name], based in the Philippines ("Contractor")

1. Scope of Work

The Contractor agrees to provide virtual assistant services as described below:

[List specific tasks — e.g., email management, scheduling, social media posting, customer support, bookkeeping, etc.]

Hours: [e.g., 20 hours per week / 40 hours per week]

Schedule: [e.g., Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm PHT / flexible async]

2. Compensation

The Client agrees to pay the Contractor at a rate of [e.g., $7/hour or $600/month], payable [weekly / bi-weekly / monthly] via [Wise / PayPal / Payoneer / bank transfer].

Payment will be made on or before [e.g., every Friday / the 1st and 15th of the month]. Late payments beyond 5 business days will be communicated in advance by the Client.

3. Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client. The Contractor is responsible for their own taxes, social contributions, and any government-mandated obligations in the Philippines. The Client will not withhold taxes or provide employee benefits.

4. Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees to keep all Client information, business data, client lists, and proprietary materials strictly confidential during and after this engagement. The Contractor will not share, sell, or disclose any such information to third parties without written consent from the Client.

5. Intellectual Property

All work, content, designs, documents, or deliverables created by the Contractor in the course of this engagement are the sole property of the Client. The Contractor waives any claim to ownership of work produced under this Agreement.

6. Communication and Availability

The Contractor agrees to be reachable via [e.g., Slack / Email / WhatsApp] during agreed working hours. The Contractor will notify the Client at least [24 / 48] hours in advance of any planned absence or unavailability.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with [7 / 14 / 30] days' written notice. The Client reserves the right to terminate immediately for cause (e.g., breach of confidentiality, fraud, or repeated non-performance). Upon termination, the Contractor will be paid for all work completed up to the termination date.

8. Governing Terms

Both parties agree to resolve any disputes in good faith before seeking formal legal remedies. This Agreement reflects the full understanding between both parties and supersedes any prior verbal or written arrangements.

How to Use This Template

  1. Copy the contract above into a Google Doc or Word document
  2. Fill in all highlighted fields — marked in yellow in the template
  3. Share with your VA via Google Docs (request comment or e-signature) or use a free tool like DocuSign or HelloSign
  4. Both parties sign — digital signatures are legally valid in most jurisdictions
  5. Save a copy to your records and send one to your VA

Tip: Even if you trust your VA completely, a signed contract protects them too — it confirms the rate, the scope, and the payment schedule in writing. Filipino professionals appreciate the professionalism it signals.

Common Questions About Hiring Filipino VAs on Contract

Do I need to pay Philippine taxes for my VA?

Generally, no. When hiring a Filipino worker as an independent contractor from outside the Philippines, the tax obligation falls on the contractor in the Philippines, not the foreign employer. However, tax situations vary — if you're establishing a Philippine entity or have significant operations there, consult a local accountant.

What currency should I pay in?

USD is the most common currency for international VA arrangements with Filipino workers. Most use Wise (formerly TransferWise), Payoneer, or PayPal to receive payments. Agree on the currency upfront and specify it in the contract.

Is a 13th month bonus required?

The 13th month pay is mandatory for employees under Philippine labor law — but independent contractors are not covered by the same rules. That said, many employers choose to give a 13th month bonus as a goodwill gesture. It significantly improves loyalty and retention.

How long should the contract be?

Most VA contracts are open-ended (no fixed end date) with a termination notice clause, which is what this template uses. This is more practical than fixed-term contracts for ongoing work relationships.
