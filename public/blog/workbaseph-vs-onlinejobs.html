This is an honest breakdown. We'll cover pricing, candidate quality, ease of use, and the specific situations where each platform makes more sense.

Pricing: What Do You Actually Pay?

OnlineJobs.ph

OnlineJobs.ph runs on a monthly subscription model. To contact candidates or post jobs, you need to pay $69/month (basic) or $99/month (pro). If you hire within the first month, that's fine — but if your search takes longer, costs add up quickly. There's no per-hire fee, which is good for high-volume hiring, but the monthly commitment is a barrier for employers who only hire occasionally.

WorkBase PH

WorkBase PH charges a flat $18 fee for 1 job post. You get full access to every applicant's profile and contact details from day one — no monthly subscription, no per-message fees. For employers who hire once or twice a year, this is significantly cheaper. For those hiring at scale every month, the comparison narrows.

Bottom line on pricing: If you hire fewer than 3 times per year, WorkBase PH is almost certainly cheaper. If you're constantly hiring and need to browse a huge pool of passive candidates, OnlineJobs.ph's subscription starts to make more sense.

Candidate Quality and Vetting

OnlineJobs.ph

OnlineJobs.ph has millions of registered profiles — which sounds like a good thing until you're wading through hundreds of unqualified applicants. Profiles are self-reported, meaning there's no standardized vetting process. The platform does offer ID verification as an optional add-on, but it's not required. Scam reports from freelancers (fake job posts, non-payment) are a recurring complaint in the platform's community forums.

WorkBase PH

WorkBase PH takes a smaller, curated approach. Every specialist records a video and audio introduction and completes an internet speed test and a work-style assessment — so you can judge communication style and personality, not just a self-written CV. Employers are also verified — so freelancers are protected from scam job posts. The trade-off is a smaller pool. But for most employers, 10 candidates you've seen and heard beat 500 unfiltered ones.

Ease of Use

OnlineJobs.ph

The platform has a traditional job board feel — you search, filter, and browse profiles manually. It works, but it takes time. Some employers spend days sifting through applications to find candidates worth interviewing. The interface is functional but dated compared to newer platforms.

WorkBase PH

WorkBase PH uses AI-powered matching to surface the most relevant candidates based on your job post. Instead of browsing manually, you receive matched profiles. For time-strapped employers, this shortcut is valuable — most employers can move from posting to shortlisting in under 24 hours.

Which Platform Should You Choose?

Choose WorkBase PH if: You hire occasionally, want candidates you can see and hear without the manual search, and prefer a flat fee over a monthly subscription. Especially good for first-time employers or those who've been burned by scam profiles elsewhere.

Choose OnlineJobs.ph if: You need to hire at high volume month after month, want the largest possible pool of candidates to browse passively, or are comfortable doing your own screening from scratch.

The Honest Truth

OnlineJobs.ph is the established player with a massive candidate database built over many years. If you want the widest possible net and don't mind paying monthly and doing your own vetting, it works.

WorkBase PH is built for employers who want the process to be faster, safer, and simpler — fewer candidates, but better ones, with less time wasted on screening. The flat-fee model also makes it significantly more accessible for small businesses and first-time remote employers.

Neither platform is objectively "better" — they're built for different hiring styles. The right one depends on how you work.

The Real Cost of "Cheaper" Hiring

OnlineJobs.ph is often described as the cheaper option because its monthly subscription is a familiar cost model. But the full cost comparison is more nuanced.

Consider a typical hire on OnlineJobs.ph:

  • $69–$99/month subscription
  • You receive 50–150 applications for a standard role
  • You spend 5–10 hours reviewing profiles and filtering
  • You run 5–10 initial screening interviews before finding a shortlist
  • Total time investment before making an offer: 15–25 hours over 2–4 weeks

If your time is worth $50/hour, that's $750–$1,250 in time cost — on top of the subscription fee. For a hire that doesn't work out and requires starting over, that cost doubles.

WorkBase PH's model is designed to compress that timeline. The matching and video intros mean most employers review 5–10 genuinely relevant candidates rather than 100 unfiltered ones. For time-constrained business owners, the economics shift decisively — even if the platform fee is slightly higher for heavy users.

When OnlineJobs.ph Is the Right Choice

To be fair: there are specific situations where OnlineJobs.ph is the better tool.

  • You're hiring at high volume. If you post 3–5 roles per month, OnlineJobs.ph's unlimited posting model becomes cost-effective. WorkBase PH's per-post or subscription plans may cost more at that volume.
  • You want to browse passive candidates. OnlineJobs.ph lets you search its full database and reach out to candidates who haven't applied to your job — useful if you're hiring for a niche role and want to headhunt rather than wait for applicants.
  • You have a proven screening system. Experienced remote employers who have built their own application forms, skills tests, and filters can extract excellent candidates from OnlineJobs.ph's volume. The platform's limitations become less relevant when you compensate with your own process.
  • You're looking for very junior roles at the lowest cost. For basic data entry or simple admin tasks where skills requirements are minimal, the volume on OnlineJobs.ph gives you more price competition among candidates.

When WorkBase PH Is the Right Choice

  • You're hiring for the first time. The video intros and matching remove much of the guesswork — the platform surfaces relevant candidates and lets you see and hear them up front.
  • You've been burned by scam profiles or unresponsive candidates before. Employer verification, plus a video and audio intro from every candidate, dramatically reduces this risk.
  • You need to fill a role quickly. The matching system means you can go from job post to shortlist in under 24 hours rather than spending a week sorting applications.
  • You want to assess communication before an interview. Video and audio introductions let you evaluate candidates before scheduling calls — a time-saver that OnlineJobs.ph simply doesn't offer.
  • You hire occasionally (1–3 times per year). A flat $18 starter fee is far more cost-effective than paying $69–$99/month for a platform you only need once or twice a year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use WorkBase PH and OnlineJobs.ph at the same time?

Yes, and many employers do. Running parallel searches on both platforms maximises candidate reach. WorkBase PH will surface matched candidates with video intros quickly; OnlineJobs.ph gives you additional volume to search through if you want to be thorough. Just be prepared to manage applications from two sources.

Is OnlineJobs.ph still worth using in 2026?

Yes — for the right employer. Its database is unmatched in size and it's been the dominant platform in this space for over 15 years. The limitations (no vetting, manual search, no video introductions) are real, but experienced employers who know how to screen have hired excellent long-term team members through it. The key is going in with a good screening process, not assuming the platform does the filtering for you.

Does OnlineJobs.ph verify employers?

No — anyone can post a job on OnlineJobs.ph without verification. This is one of the most frequently cited complaints from Filipino workers on the platform — scam job posts (asking for upfront fees, fake companies, non-payment) are a persistent problem. WorkBase PH verifies all employers before they can post, which is one reason its specialist pool tends to include more experienced, professionally-minded workers who aren't willing to take risks with unverified employers.

What's the cancellation policy on OnlineJobs.ph?

OnlineJobs.ph subscriptions are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time — you retain access until the end of your paid period. There are no long-term contracts. WorkBase PH's Starter plan is a one-time flat fee with no subscription required, so there's nothing to cancel.

Which platform do Filipino workers prefer?

It depends on the worker. Established Filipino professionals with 3+ years of remote experience often prefer platforms with employer verification because they've encountered scam posts on open boards. Newer entrants to remote work tend to apply everywhere, including open job boards. If your goal is attracting experienced, discerning candidates, a platform that signals employer legitimacy (like WorkBase PH) tends to pull a stronger applicant pool for professional roles.
