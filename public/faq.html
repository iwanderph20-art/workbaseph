For Employers

WorkBase PH is a smart-matching platform that connects Filipino remote talent with employers — not a traditional job board where you post and pray.

Instead of sorting through every applicant on the internet, WorkBase PH uses intelligent matching to surface only the candidates who genuinely fit your role — based on skills, experience level, availability, and working style.

On pricing: we offer a flat $15 starter fee for one-off hiring, or affordable monthly plans starting at $49. No commissions, no per-hire fees, no surprises.

Three self-service plans to choose from:

Starter — $15 flat: Up to 2 active job posts. Unlimited applications, free messaging, candidate profiles, standard listing visibility, and built-in interview scheduling. AI Candidate Audit available as a $15 add-on.

Essential — $49/month (7-day free trial): Up to 10 active job posts, unlimited applicants, unlimited messaging, applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and Priority Listing visibility. Annual plan: $490/year (2 months free).

Pro — $79/month (7-day free trial): Unlimited job posts, everything in Essential, plus 3 team seats, Unlimited AI Profile Audits, and Priority Support. Annual plan: $790/year (2 months free).

The AI Candidate Audit is a $15 add-on available on Starter and Essential plans (included free and unlimited on Pro). WorkBase AI analyzes all applicants against your job description and categorizes each one as a match or mismatch, with a brief 3-word reason for each mismatch (e.g., "Low Experience", "Missing Tool Skill").

Mismatched applicants are archived — not deleted — and you can view them anytime. Qualified candidates stay front and center on your dashboard.

For employers: Verification is required for long-term hiring and optional for project-based hiring. It's a one-time process. Any one of the following is accepted (must include your business name and address):

  • Utility bill (internet, electricity, or water — addressed to your company)
  • Business License or Articles of Incorporation
  • Tax Permit or Insurance Document
  • Lease or Franchise Agreement
  • Industry-specific or government-issued license

For specialists: No mandatory verification to get started. Your profile is reviewed by our team before appearing in employer matches.

For Specialists

Yes — 100% free. WorkBase PH earns exclusively from employer subscription fees. We never charge specialists a registration fee, commission, or platform fee. Every peso you earn through a WorkBase PH match belongs entirely to you.

Once your profile is complete — including your personality video, assessment results, and verified readiness documents — you become discoverable to employers. You can also browse open job posts and apply directly.

Employers review applicants, watch your video introduction, and reach out through the built-in inbox to schedule an interview. The stronger and more complete your profile, the more likely employers are to choose you.

The Personality Assessment is a 20-question work-style profile that reveals your communication strengths, work preferences, and best-fit employer type. Your results appear permanently on your profile and are visible to every employer who views you.

It cannot be re-taken or removed once submitted — so take your time and answer honestly.

Record a 5–10 minute personality reel using Loom, YouTube (unlisted), or Vocaroo. Talk about who you are, how you work, what you're great at, and what kind of employer or project you're looking for. Be real — authenticity wins.

Make sure your audio is clear and your environment is clean and professional.

When an employer wants to interview you, they send two available time slots directly to your Inbox inside the WorkBase PH dashboard. Open your Inbox, review both options, and click Confirm on the one that works best.

A Jitsi video meeting link is automatically generated and shared with both of you. No email chains, no scheduling tools — everything happens inside the platform.
