Specimen Collection
Collection Process Guide
Medicare 2 SST  ·  1 LAVENDER  ·  1 URINE CUP — Always, unless specified otherwise
1
Call the Facility
Inform them you're on the way and share your ETA.
2
Prioritize & Collect
Collect patients on a program that day first, then perform collection on the remaining residents on the list.
3
Transport to Lab
Take the collection to Juno Tech Lab.
4
Sort & Reference Lab Lockbox
Sort collections and place in the Reference Lab Lockbox for pickup.
5
Notify Logistics
Inform Logistics that specimens are in the Reference Lab Lockbox prior to clocking out.
6
Clean Work Area
Organize and clean your work area before clocking out.
Medi-Cal 1 SST  ·  1 LAVENDER (no urine cup) — Always, unless specified otherwise
YES
1
Finish the Requisitions Before going to the facility — ideally the day before collection.
↓
2
Collection at the Facility Perform the specimen collection on-site.
↓
3
Leave at Quest Lockbox Leave specimens with requisitions in the Quest lockbox.

No lockbox? Hand to admin — ask them to set aside for Quest pickup.
↓
4
Notify Logistics Inform Logistics that specimens have been left in the Quest dropbox.
NO
1
Collect at the Facility Perform the collection on-site.
↓
2
Bring to the Lab Take the collection with you to the lab.
↓
