Questions

You probably want to know.

Is the talent actually good quality?
Most specialists record a video or audio intro, run an internet speed test, and complete a work-style assessment. You get a feel for how they communicate and their personality before you message — more than most platforms show you even after you hire.
I've never hired remotely before. Where do I start?
Post a job — describe the role, hours, and pay range. Our AI surfaces matched candidates for you. Review profiles and video intros, then message whoever fits. Most first-timers make their first hire within a week.
How do I pay a Filipino freelancer?
Most employers use Wise, Payoneer, or direct bank transfer — fast, low-fee, and widely used in the Philippines. You agree on a rate directly with your hire. WorkBase PH takes no cut of wages.
Do I need a monthly subscription?
No. All-Access is $29 one-time for 1 job post — no subscription, no recurring charges. The listing's 30-day window starts on your purchase date (post soon after buying to make the most of it), then pauses; post another ($29) whenever you're hiring again. Every tool is included in the flat fee.
How is this different from OnlineJobs.ph?
OnlineJobs.ph is a raw job board — no video intros, no matching, and you pay monthly just to see contact details. WorkBase PH gives most specialists a video or audio intro, AI-matches to your role, and gives you full access from the start.
How quickly can I find someone?
Most employers get their first matched shortlist within 24 hours of posting. Average time from post to offer: 3–7 days.
Is it safe to hire from the Philippines?
Yes — millions of global businesses do it every year. WorkBase PH verifies every employer, and most specialists record a video or audio intro so you know who you're hiring. Use a simple contractor agreement and pay via Wise or Payoneer.
What roles can I hire for?
VAs, executive assistants, bookkeepers, customer support, social media, content writers, video editors, graphic designers, developers, and more. If it can be done remotely, there's a Filipino specialist for it.
