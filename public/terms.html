Scope of Service

WorkBase PH is an intelligent talent-matching platform that connects Employers with Filipino remote Specialists through smart-matching technology. Unlike a traditional job board, WorkBase PH curates and surfaces the right talent directly to employers — based on skills, experience, and role fit.

WorkBase PH earns revenue exclusively from Employer subscription plans. Specialists pay nothing and are never charged a commission or platform fee. Every peso earned by a Specialist through a WorkBase PH engagement belongs entirely to them.

2

Employer Access & Pricing Plans

WorkBase PH offers three self-service hiring plans. All plan fees are charged in USD and processed via PayPal.

Starter — $18 flat

Up to 2 active job posts, each active for 30 days from posting. After 30 days a listing is automatically paused and hidden from candidates; you may repost (a new Starter purchase) or upgrade to a subscription to keep it live. Includes unlimited applicants, free messaging, candidate profiles, standard listing visibility, and built-in interview scheduling. AI Candidate Audit available as a $15 add-on per post. Starter fees are non-refundable.

Essential — $49/month (Cancel Anytime)

Up to 10 active job posts, unlimited applicants, unlimited messaging, applicant tracking (pipeline view), interview scheduling, and Priority Listing visibility. Includes a 7-day free trial. Annual plan: $490/year (2 months free). Fees are non-refundable once the billing period starts.

Pro — $79/month (Cancel Anytime)

Unlimited job posts, everything in Essential, plus 3 team seats, Unlimited AI Profile Audits, and Priority Support. Includes a 7-day free trial. Annual plan: $790/year (2 months free). Fees are non-refundable once the billing period starts.

2a

AI Candidate Audit Add-On

The AI Candidate Audit is a one-time, per-job-post add-on available to Starter and Essential subscribers at $15 per audit. WorkBase PH's AI analyzes applicant profiles against the Job Description and categorizes each applicant as a match or mismatch, with a brief reason for each mismatch.

Mismatched applicants are archived (not deleted) and remain viewable at any time. Pro plan subscribers receive unlimited AI Candidate Audits at no additional cost. All hiring decisions remain the sole responsibility of the Employer. AI Audit fees are non-refundable once processed.
