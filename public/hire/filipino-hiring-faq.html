Costs

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino virtual assistant?

Most Filipino VAs cost about $400–$800/month full-time for general support and $800–$1,600/month for specialized or senior roles. Part-time rates run roughly $5–$10/hour — about 70–80% less than a comparable US hire. On WorkBase PH you pay a flat $29 to post the job and the VA's agreed rate directly, with no commission. How much does a Filipino bookkeeper cost?

A Filipino bookkeeper costs $400–$600/month entry-level, $600–$900 mid-level, and $900–$1,800 for senior bookkeepers or CPAs with US/AU tax experience — roughly 70–85% less than a US bookkeeper ($3,500–$5,500/month). More in the bookkeeper cost guide or on the hire a bookkeeper page.

Should I hire a Filipino VA full-time or part-time?

Part-time (10–20 hours/week) suits lower, steady workloads; full-time makes sense once there's enough daily work or you want a dedicated person. A practical approach is to start part-time and expand to full-time as the workload proves out.

How do I screen a Filipino VA?

Be specific in the job post, watch the candidate's video and audio intro to judge communication, give a small paid task relevant to the role, check an internet speed test (20+ Mbps recommended), and run a short video interview. Testing the actual work beats relying on a resume.

Payments & legal

How do I pay a Filipino virtual assistant?

Most employers use Wise, Payoneer, or direct bank transfer — all low-fee and widely used in the Philippines. You agree the rate directly with your hire and pay on your schedule. WorkBase PH takes no commission from the wages you pay. More detail: how to pay a Filipino VA.

Do I owe taxes or benefits when hiring a Filipino contractor?

Filipino remote workers are typically independent contractors: you generally don't withhold Philippine taxes or provide local benefits, and they handle their own taxes. Use a simple written contractor agreement. This is general information, not legal or tax advice — check with a professional for your situation.

Can Filipino workers cover US or Australian business hours?

Yes. Many Filipino remote workers come from the BPO industry and routinely work US or Australian hours. State the hours you need in the job post and confirm the candidate can sustain that schedule long-term.
