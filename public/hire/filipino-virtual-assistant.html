Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino virtual assistant?

In 2026, a Filipino VA typically costs $300–$500/month entry-level, $500–$800 mid-level, and $800–$1,200 for a senior or specialized assistant. Executive and technical VAs run $1,000–$1,500 — roughly 70–80% less than a US-based assistant, who averages $3,000–$4,500/month. Most work full-time for a flat monthly rate.

What tasks can a Filipino virtual assistant handle?

A general VA handles email and calendar management, appointment scheduling, data entry, internet research, travel booking, CRM updates, basic bookkeeping, social media scheduling, and customer support. Specialized VAs also cover e-commerce store management, executive support, and lead generation. Define the exact tasks in your job post and screen for the tools you use.

Full-time or part-time — which should I hire?

Most Filipino VAs prefer full-time roles (40 hours/week) with a stable monthly salary, and full-time hires tend to be more committed and easier to retain. Part-time (20 hours/week) works well for a lighter, well-defined workload. You can also start part-time and move to full-time as the relationship proves out.
