Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino social media manager?

In 2026, a Filipino social media manager typically costs $300–$600/month for an entry-level scheduler, $600–$1,000 mid-level, and $1,000–$1,800 for a senior manager or strategist. Add paid-ads and campaign experience and you're at $1,200–$2,000 — roughly 60–80% less than a US-based social media manager, who averages $4,000–$6,000/month.

What's the difference between a scheduler and a strategist?

A scheduler executes a plan you provide — creating graphics, writing captions, and posting on a calendar. A strategist owns the outcome: they set the content strategy, choose channels, analyze performance, and adjust. If you already know what to post, hire a scheduler. If you want someone to grow the account, hire a strategist and expect to pay $1,000+/month.

Which platforms and tools do Filipino social media managers know?

Most are fluent across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, and X, plus scheduling tools like Meta Business Suite, Buffer, Hootsuite, and Later. Many also use Canva and CapCut for graphics and short-form video, and can run Meta and TikTok ads. Name your platforms and tools in the job post and screen for them.
