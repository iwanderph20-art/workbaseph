Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino graphic designer?

In 2026, a Filipino graphic designer typically costs $350–$600/month for an entry-level designer, $600–$1,100 mid-level, and $1,100–$1,800 for a senior designer. A brand or UI/UX specialist runs $1,500–$2,200 — roughly 60–80% less than a US-based graphic designer, who averages $4,500–$6,500/month.

What design tools do Filipino graphic designers use?

Most are fluent in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, plus Canva for fast social graphics and Figma for UI and web design. Many also handle After Effects for motion, and export production-ready files for print and web. Ask for their tool stack and match it to your project.

Should I always review a designer's portfolio?

Yes — a portfolio is the single best predictor of fit for a designer. Look for range, consistency, and work in your industry or style. On WorkBase PH, designers include portfolio links in their profile, and you can watch a video intro to hear how they think about a brief before you message.
