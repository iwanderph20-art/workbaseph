Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino data entry specialist?

In 2026, a Filipino data entry specialist typically costs $250–$450/month for an entry-level worker, $450–$700 mid-level, and $700–$1,000 for a senior specialist who handles CRM cleanup, research, and light automation — roughly 60–80% less than a US-based data entry clerk, who averages $2,800–$3,600/month.

What software do Filipino data entry specialists use?

Most are fluent in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets, plus Airtable and common CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho. Many also handle PDF-to-Excel conversion, e-commerce product listing on Shopify and Amazon, and data cleanup with formulas, lookups, and pivot tables. Name your tools in the job post and screen for them.

How do I make sure data entry work is accurate?

Ask for a short paid test task on real sample data and check the error rate. Good specialists build in verification steps — double entry, validation rules, or checksum totals — and flag anything ambiguous instead of guessing.
