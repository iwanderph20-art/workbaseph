Frequently asked questions

How much does a Filipino real estate virtual assistant cost?

In 2026, a general real estate VA handling CRM updates, data entry, and basic admin typically costs $400–$700/month full-time. A cold-calling or lead-gen specialist running an ISA-style workflow costs $600–$1,000, a transaction coordinator managing contract-to-close paperwork runs $700–$1,100, and a senior REVA or ISA team lead costs $1,000–$1,600 — roughly 70–80% less than a US-based equivalent.

Can a Filipino VA legally cold call for real estate in the US?

Yes. A real estate VA dials on behalf of your licensed brokerage using your scripts and systems — they aren't practicing real estate or acting as an agent, so no US real estate license is required for prospecting or appointment setting. Train them on Do Not Call list compliance and use a TCPA-compliant dialer, since those rules apply regardless of where the caller is based.

What real estate software should my VA know?

Common tools include CRMs like Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, and Podio; dialers such as Mojo or REISimpli; MLS access for comps and listing research; and e-signature/TC tools like Skyslope or Dotloop. Most experienced Filipino REVAs have touched at least two or three of these already and pick up a new stack quickly.

How do I train a REVA on my specific market and scripts?

Start with a short script and objection-handling document, record a few sample calls or role-plays, and have them shadow your calls for the first few days before dialing live. Give them your target list criteria (area, property type, motivation) clearly defined, and review recorded calls weekly for the first month to correct tone and pacing early.
