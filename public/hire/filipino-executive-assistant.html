Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between an executive assistant and a virtual assistant?

A general VA executes defined tasks you assign. An executive assistant operates one level up: they manage your calendar and inbox with judgment, prioritize on your behalf, coordinate with clients and your team, and anticipate what you need before you ask. You're hiring for judgment and discretion, which is why an EA costs more and is screened differently.

How much does a Filipino executive assistant cost?

In 2026, a capable Filipino EA typically costs $600–$1,000/month for solid mid-level support and $1,000–$1,600 for a senior EA. That's roughly 70–80% less than a US-based EA, who commonly costs $5,000–$7,000/month plus benefits.

Can a Filipino EA work in my time zone?

Yes. Many Filipino EAs are accustomed to working US, UK, or Australian hours so they can manage your calendar and inbox in real time. Agree on the core hours you need covered and confirm the candidate can sustain that schedule long-term.

Is it safe to give an executive assistant access to my email and calendar?

Yes, with standard precautions. Use delegated access (Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 both support inbox and calendar delegation) rather than sharing your password, enable two-factor authentication, and include a confidentiality clause in the contract. Start with limited scope and expand as trust builds.
