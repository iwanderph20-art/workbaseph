Frequently asked questions

How much does a Filipino Shopify virtual assistant cost?

In 2026, a general Shopify VA handling product uploads, order support, and store admin typically costs $400–$700/month full-time. A VA who also does design, apps, and light theme edits runs $700–$1,100, and a technical Shopify specialist comfortable with Liquid costs $1,000–$1,600 — roughly 70–80% less than a US-based equivalent.

What can a Shopify VA actually do in my store?

A capable Shopify VA can add and optimize products, manage inventory and collections, process orders, returns, and refunds, respond to customer inquiries, install and configure apps, make simple theme and content edits, build basic email flows in Klaviyo, and keep your product data and reporting clean. Deep custom development is a specialist skill you should screen for separately.

Do I need a technical Shopify developer or just a VA?

Most stores need a VA, not a developer. Day-to-day e-commerce is product management, order support, content updates, and app configuration — all within a general Shopify VA's range. Hire a developer only for custom theme work, complex Liquid, or building functionality apps don't provide.

Is it safe to give a Shopify VA access to my store?

Yes, using Shopify's staff accounts with granular permissions rather than sharing your owner login. Grant only the sections the role needs (for example products and orders but not payouts or account settings), enable two-factor authentication, and expand access as trust builds. Never share bank or payment provider credentials.
