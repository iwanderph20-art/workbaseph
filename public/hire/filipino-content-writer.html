Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a Filipino content writer?

In 2026, a Filipino content writer typically costs $350–$600/month for an entry-level writer, $600–$1,000 mid-level, and $1,000–$1,600 for a senior writer or editor. An SEO or technical specialist runs $1,200–$2,000 — roughly 60–80% less than a US-based content writer, who averages $4,000–$6,000/month.

Is Filipino writers' English good enough for native audiences?

The Philippines is one of the largest English-speaking countries in the world, and English is a language of instruction and business there. Most professional Filipino writers produce clean, native-sounding copy for US, UK, and Australian audiences. Always review writing samples and assign a short paid test to confirm tone and style fit.

Can Filipino content writers do SEO writing?

Yes. Many Filipino writers are experienced with keyword research, on-page SEO, search intent, and tools like Surfer, Clearscope, and Google Search Console. If SEO matters, screen for it specifically — ask for a ranking article they wrote and how they approached the brief.
