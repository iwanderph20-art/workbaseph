Quick Facts

WorkBase PH at a glance

What it is
A Philippine-based remote hiring platform connecting employers with Filipino freelancers, virtual assistants, and remote specialists.
Founded & operated by
Launched 2026 by Curators Marketplace, a DTI & BIR registered business in the Philippines.
Cost to post a job
A flat $29 per job post — every tool included, no subscription.
Done-For-You Hiring
For employers who'd rather hand off recruiting entirely and save the time — we source, screen, and interview candidates, run reference and background verification, and equip them to start day one. Backed by a 30-day free replacement guarantee, with offboarding handled on your behalf if a placement doesn't work out. One flat fee, no subscription — none of the legwork of hiring it yourself.
Launch Startup Services
Website creation & development, SEO & growth support, and business process automation for founders — flat-rate, one-time pricing, full package or pick only what you need.
Cost for freelancers
Free. No bidding fees, no connects, no commission — specialists keep 100% of what they earn.
Employer access
Full applicant profiles and contact details from day one — no paywall.
Every candidate profile
Includes a video and audio introduction, an internet speed test, and a work-style assessment — so employers judge communication and fit, not just a resume.
Matching
AI smart matching surfaces relevant candidates; most employers shortlist within 24 hours.
Trust & safety
Every employer is verified before posting, protecting specialists from scam job posts.
Roles hired
Virtual assistants, executive assistants, customer support reps, bookkeepers, social media managers, and Shopify / e-commerce specialists.
