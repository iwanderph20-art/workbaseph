Frequently asked questions

Is Team Hiring different from posting a job myself?

Yes. A regular $29 job post relies on you reviewing applicants and running interviews yourself. Team Hiring is done-for-you: our team sources, screens, and runs first-round interviews for each seat you need filled, so you're not sifting through resumes or interviewing candidates one by one — you review a ready-to-hire shortlist and make the final call.

Do you interview candidates for me?

Yes. Our team sources candidates and runs first-round interviews, which we record — so you can simply pick from the shortlist, or watch the recordings and take any candidate further with your own interview. Either way, we only introduce candidates we're confident have what it takes to succeed in the role.

Do you check references or run background checks?

Yes. We check references and run background verification on every candidate we introduce — we verify it ourselves, we don't just take a candidate's word for it.

Are candidates equipped to work right away?

Yes. We confirm each candidate has a proper workstation and the right technology for the role — including things like a noise-cancelling headset — before they start, so they're work-ready from day one, not just claiming to be.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

Whatever the role needs, we actively support your new hire through the first 30 days. If a placement still doesn't work out in that window, we'll source a replacement for that seat at no additional cost.

Who handles it if I need to let someone go later?

We do. If it's ever time to part ways with a Team Hiring placement, tell us and we'll handle the offboarding conversation directly — you don't have to have that difficult talk yourself.

How is Team Hiring priced?

A one-time payment per seat filled — no subscription, no recurring cost, ever. After your intake, we confirm the details with you and send a one-time payment request per seat.

What roles does Team Hiring cover?

Common requests include sales, intake/front desk, medical billing, customer support, IT support, and data entry — but any role in our regular hire-by-role list can be sourced in bulk. Tell us the role and headcount in the intake form.

Can Team Hiring move fast if I need people urgently?

Yes — tell us your timeline in the intake form and we'll prioritize accordingly. Team Hiring exists partly for urgent headcount needs where you don't have weeks to run a hiring process yourself.
