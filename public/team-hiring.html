Frequently asked questions

Is Team Hiring different from posting a job myself?

Yes. A regular $29 job post relies on you reviewing applicants and running interviews yourself. Team Hiring is done-for-you: our team sources, screens, and runs first-round interviews for each seat you need filled, so you're not sifting through resumes or interviewing candidates one by one — you review a ready-to-hire shortlist and make the final call.

Do you interview candidates for me?

Yes. Our team runs the sourcing and first-round interviews, so only candidates who are actually worth your time reach your shortlist. You still make the final hiring decision, but you're not the one running dozens of first interviews.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

If a Team Hiring placement doesn't work out within the first 30 days, we'll source a replacement for that seat at no additional cost.

How is Team Hiring priced?

One flat, one-time fee per hire, equal to one month's salary for that role — that's it. No subscription, no recurring cost, and no additional commission once that fee is paid. You only pay for seats you actually fill.

What roles does Team Hiring cover?

Common requests include sales, intake/front desk, medical billing, customer support, IT support, and data entry — but any role in our regular hire-by-role list can be sourced in bulk. Tell us the role and headcount in the intake form.

Can Team Hiring move fast if I need people urgently?

Yes — tell us your timeline in the intake form and we'll prioritize accordingly. Team Hiring exists partly for urgent headcount needs where you don't have weeks to run a hiring process yourself.
