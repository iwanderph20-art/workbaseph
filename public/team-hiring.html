Frequently asked questions

Is Team Hiring different from posting a job myself?

It builds on the same platform. A regular $29 job post relies on candidates applying to you. Team Hiring is for when you need several people in the same role at once — our team actively sources and shortlists candidates for each seat instead of waiting on the applicant pool, so you get a fuller slate, faster.

Do I still interview and hire the candidates myself?

Yes. We source and shortlist; you review each candidate's video and audio intro, message and interview whoever you like, and make every final hiring decision yourself. WorkBase PH never hires, manages, or pays your team for you.

What roles does Team Hiring cover?

Common requests include sales, intake/front desk, medical billing, customer support, IT support, and data entry — but any role in our regular hire-by-role list can be sourced in bulk. Tell us the role and headcount in the intake form.

How is Team Hiring priced?

It depends on the role and how many people you need, so we'd rather quote accurately than guess here. Fill out the intake form and our team will follow up with a tailored quote.
