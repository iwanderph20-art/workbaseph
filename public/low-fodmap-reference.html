Low FODMAP Food Reference

Safe foods in moderate/typical serving sizes — always check portion for your tolerance

🍓 Fruits

  • Banana (firm/unripe)
  • Blueberries
  • Strawberries
  • Grapes
  • Kiwi
  • Mandarin orange
  • Pineapple
  • Cantaloupe
  • Papaya
  • Raspberries
  • Passion fruit
  • Lemon / Lime

🥦 Vegetables

  • Bell pepper
  • Carrot
  • Cucumber
  • Zucchini
  • Eggplant
  • Green beans
  • Kale
  • Spinach
  • Tomato (common)
  • Potato
  • Sweet potato (small)
  • Bok choy
  • Bean sprouts
  • Corn (half cob)
  • Lettuce (all types)
  • Pumpkin (small serve)

🌾 Grains & Starches

  • White rice
  • Brown rice
  • Oats (rolled, ½ cup)
  • Gluten-free bread
  • Sourdough (spelt)
  • Rice cakes
  • Corn tortilla
  • Quinoa
  • Buckwheat
  • Millet
  • Polenta
  • Rice noodles
  • Tapioca

🥩 Protein

  • Chicken
  • Turkey
  • Beef
  • Pork
  • Lamb
  • Fish (all types)
  • Shrimp / Prawn
  • Eggs
  • Tofu (firm)
  • Tempeh
  • Canned tuna
  • Canned salmon

🥛 Dairy & Alternatives

  • Lactose-free milk
  • Almond milk (plain)
  • Rice milk
  • Oat milk (plain)
  • Hard cheese (cheddar, parmesan)
  • Brie / Camembert
  • Lactose-free yogurt
  • Lactose-free ice cream
  • Butter

🥜 Nuts & Seeds

  • Macadamia (10–15)
  • Pecans (10)
  • Walnuts (10)
  • Peanuts (32g)
  • Pine nuts (14g)
  • Chia seeds (2 tbsp)
  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Sunflower seeds
  • Sesame seeds
  • Hemp seeds

🧂 Condiments & Flavors

  • Olive oil
  • Soy sauce (small amt)
  • Tamari
  • Maple syrup
  • White/brown sugar
  • Mustard (plain)
  • Mayonnaise
  • Vinegar (rice, balsamic)
  • Salt & pepper
  • Most fresh herbs
  • Chili (fresh, small amt)
  • Ginger
  • Turmeric
  • Garlic-infused oil*

🫖 Drinks

  • Water
  • Black coffee
  • Green tea
  • Peppermint tea
  • White tea
  • Ginger tea
  • Sparkling water
  • Orange juice (small)
  • Tomato juice (small)

High FODMAP — Avoid or Limit

Fruits

  • Apple
  • Pear
  • Mango
  • Watermelon
  • Cherries
  • Peach / Nectarine
  • Dried fruits

Vegetables

  • Onion (all forms)
  • Garlic
  • Cauliflower
  • Mushrooms
  • Asparagus
  • Artichoke
  • Leek (white part)

Grains & Legumes

  • Wheat bread
  • Rye bread
  • Barley
  • Chickpeas
  • Lentils
  • Kidney beans
  • Baked beans

Dairy

  • Cow's milk
  • Soft cheese (ricotta)
  • Yogurt (regular)
  • Ice cream (regular)
  • Custard

Sweeteners

  • Honey
  • High-fructose corn syrup
  • Agave
  • Sorbitol (xylitol, mannitol)
  • Sugar-free gum/candy

* Garlic-infused oil is safe as FODMAPs don't transfer to oil. | Serving size matters — some foods are low FODMAP only in small amounts.

Not medical advice. Based on Monash University FODMAP guidelines. Consult a dietitian for personalized guidance.
